05 · QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Do I need to have a script?

It helps, but no. Send me the product, the offer and who it's for, and I'll write hook options for you to approve first.

Are the people in the ads real?

No. The actors and creators are AI-generated characters, locked to a consistent look across the whole ad and every hook.

How many hooks do I get?

Usually 3 to 5 hooks per ad on the same body, so you can test openings without paying for a whole new video.

What styles can you make?

AI animation (skeleton stories and 3D product stories), Pixar-style AI song ads, voiceover + B-roll product ads, short drama ads, creator-style UGC with different avatars, and static image ads.

Why Claude MCP?

It connects research, writing and the video models in one pipeline. Hooks, prompts and character sheets stay consistent, and I spend my time on the edit instead of copy-pasting prompts.

Can I download the samples?

The samples here are view-only. Client work is delivered at full resolution in a private Google Drive folder.
